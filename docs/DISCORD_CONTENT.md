## how-to-read-picks

**Market:** exactly what the pick covers—team moneyline, spread, total, or a
specified player prop. Coverage depends on the sport and model actually available.

**Posted price:** the sportsbook and odds observed at the stated time.
**Play-to price:** the worst odds we would accept for that exact pick.
**Units:** a relative stake suggestion; choose your own unit size and budget.
**Status:** a published pick, an update, a watchlist candidate, or research.

A WATCH or RESEARCH label is not a bet recommendation. A changed line is a new
decision; don't assume an old recommendation applies. Passing is part of the process.
