# Your JABBAZI VIP app

1. In Discord, use `/vip` (private reply) or `!vip` (DM/private fallback). Open the link once within five minutes. It is private: do not forward it.
2. Home shows available research and official recommendations separately. An empty official card or Top 10 list is a valid outcome.
3. Sports opens available game/player markets. Use search, sport/state filters and sort, then open a card for provenance, price timestamps, fair price and related markets.
4. Daily Cheat Sheets shows the exact frozen morning moneyline sheet used by Discord. Select a sport or past date. Historical prices are never presented as current prices.
5. What Changed compares that morning baseline with later observed moneyline prices/model estimates. It does not rewrite the morning record.
6. Add research to Watchlist. Optionally set a minimum decimal price. The private inbox reports observed state changes; a target being met is not approval to bet. Alerts are in-app, not phone push notifications.
7. More → My Board lets you choose sports, set your unit size and optionally store bankroll. The starting reference is $30 per unit. Personal settings do not change model probabilities.
8. Performance reports authentic official/scanner-origin ledger data, with provenance. User sprinkles and community tickets do not count toward official results.

WATCH is research requiring review. PASS means no supported play. PRICE CHECK means the quoted price is not verified current. QUARANTINED/INVALIDATED must not be treated as actionable. BET NOW appears only for a current canonical official recommendation that clears existing gates.

Fair price is the price implied by the stated model probability; an experimental model's fair price is experimental too. Maximum playable price appears only when the backend supplies one. No wager is safe or guaranteed.

Sessions expire after 15 minutes. Ask Discord for a new `/vip` link. Role removal revokes protected API access after a bounded 30-second authorization cache; the browser checks every 30 seconds. When offline, live research is hidden. Refresh reads saved research and never starts a paid scan.
